## Length: brief

The whole Script is under 100 words: the headline sentence, then one paragraph of three or four sentences. That is the one-minute version, whatever the Response's length. Say what was decided, done, or found, the reason that matters most, and anything the listener must act on. Everything else goes: examples, alternatives, numbers that do not change the decision, supporting detail. If the Response itself is under 100 words, the Script is simply all of it.
